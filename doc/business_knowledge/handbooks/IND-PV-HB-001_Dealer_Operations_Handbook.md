## 1. Purpose

This handbook defines **day-to-day dealer operating standards** for India passenger-vehicle retail: booking discipline, handover quality, stock care, and customer communication. It is **not** a metric glossary — use the semantic glossary for SQL column definitions. Use this handbook when staff ask *how we operate*, not *what the numbers are*.

## 2. Retail booking & recognition

A unit counts toward **Units Sold** only when ALL of the following are true on the same calendar day:

1. Customer KYC complete (PAN or Aadhaar on file)  
2. Full payment or approved finance disbursal  
3. RTO registration initiated (temporary or permanent)  
4. Vehicle dispatched from dealer stock or plant allocation  

**Do not** recognise demo/fleet/internal transfer vehicles as retail unless SOP-001 fleet exception is approved in writing.

## 3. Handover checklist (mandatory)

Before keys are handed over, the CRM must confirm:

| Step | Owner | Record |
|---|---|---|
| PDI completed | Workshop | Digital checklist ID |
| Accessories fitted (if sold) | Sales | Invoice line match |
| EV charging demo (if electric) | EV Champion | SOP-003 sign-off |
| Insurance & RSA activated | F&I | Policy number |
| Welcome call scheduled (D+1) | CRM | CRM task |

Missed EV demo is a **Category B audit finding** in metro hubs.

## 4. Stock care & ageing

- **0–30 days:** Normal display; weekly price board refresh  
- **31–60 days:** Sales Manager daily review; colour swap allowed within zone  
- **61–90 days:** Regional approval for discount beyond policy grid  
- **90+ days:** Escalate per IND-PV-SOP-004 aged-stock threshold; liquidation plan within 14 days  

Electric vehicles: maintain SOC between 40–80% in stock; charge cycle logged weekly.

## 5. Manpower & productivity norms

| Role | Norm (metro hub) | Review cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Consultant | 8–12 retail units / month | Weekly 1:1 |
| Team Leader | 4 SCs + 15% team uplift target | Monthly |
| CRM Executive | 95% lead response < 15 min (business hours) | Daily dashboard |

Underperformance for **2 consecutive months** triggers Dealer Principal action plan; copy to Regional Head if unchanged in month 3.

## 6. Customer communication standards

- First response to digital lead: **≤ 15 minutes** (09:00–20:00 local)  
- Test drive booking confirmation: same day  
- Delivery date changes: proactive SMS + call; never silent slip beyond 3 days  
- Post-delivery NPS survey: D+7; detractors escalated to Dealer Principal within 24 h  

## 7. Festive & campaign periods

During national campaigns (Diwali, year-end, Republic Day):

1. Extra stock buffer approved at zone level (not dealer-initiated)  
2. No ad-hoc discount beyond approved grid — use exchange bonus instead  
3. Daily morning huddle (15 min): yesterday units, today appointments, stock gaps  

Refer to **IND-PV-STR-001** for FY2026 festive calendar priorities.

## 8. When to escalate to OEM regional team

| Situation | Escalate to |
|---|---|
| Supplier parts delay blocking delivery > 14 days | City Manager |
| Repeated RTO backlog in district | Regional Head |
| EV charger install delay > 7 days post-delivery | EV Network lead |
| Fraud suspicion on booking | Legal + Regional Head same day |

## 9. Document control

Review annually or after major policy change. Supersedes informal dealer circulars on booking recognition. Analytics teams: this handbook does **not** replace SQL definitions in the semantic glossary.

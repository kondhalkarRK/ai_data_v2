## 1. Strategic intent

FY2026 strategy: **“Profitable volume with EV leadership in metros.”** We grow retail share in West & South, stabilise East profitability, and double down on EV customer experience — without buying share through unapproved discounting.

This is a **business strategy document**. It does not define SQL metrics (see semantic glossary) or dealer SOP steps (see handbooks / SOPs).

## 2. Strategic pillars

### Pillar A — EV acceleration

- National EV share target **11.5%** (REG-001); stretch **12.5%** in South & West  
- Launch **EV Champion** role in every metro hub (min 1 FTE)  
- Bundle home-charger consultation in booking journey (not post-delivery)  
- Marketing: shift 35% of digital spend to EV consideration content  

### Pillar B — Metro depth, tier-2 selective expansion

- **Invest:** Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad  
- **Maintain:** Chennai, Ahmedabad, Kolkata  
- **Selective openings:** 8 new outlets in tier-2 (Indore, Coimbatore, Lucknow, Patna, Vizag, Nagpur, Jaipur satellite, Kochi satellite)  
- **No new capex** in East beyond Kolkata hub until H2 review  

### Pillar C — Disciplined festive execution

- Single national festive calendar owned by Marketing (no rogue dealer campaigns)  
- Pre-build stock **6 weeks** before Diwali; aged stock cap **8%** entering Sep  
- Exchange bonus preferred over cash discount (protect margin)  

### Pillar D — Data-driven MBR culture

- Every regional review uses Country → Zone → City → Make drill (SOP-004)  
- AI / analytics commentary must cite SOP or REG targets when discussing gaps  
- “Surprise me” insights allowed in exploration — not in board MBR without validation  

## 3. Competitive posture

| Competitor cluster | Our response |
|---|---|
| Maruti volume leaders | Win on service + delivery speed, not base price |
| Tata / Mahindra EV | Match EV demo standard; highlight total cost of ownership |
| Hyundai / Kia premium hatch | Cross-sell SUV upgrade; finance subvention capped |
| Chinese EV entrants | Monitor share in metro; no panic discounting |

## 4. Financial guardrails

- **Minimum contribution margin per unit:** ₹42,000 (blended national)  
- **Max incentive spend:** 3.2% of ex-showroom (national avg)  
- **Marketing ROI floor:** 4.5 retail units per ₹1L digital spend (tracked monthly)  

Breaches require CFO + CSO joint approval.

## 5. Organisation & capability

- Regional Heads accountable for zone P&L and REG-001 delivery  
- New **Customer Journey Office** (reporting to CMO) — lead response SLA from HB-001  
- Quarterly dealer certification on EV handover (SOP-003 + HB-001 module)  

## 6. Risk register (top 5)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| EV supply constraint | Alternate allocation pool; transparent customer comms |
| RTO regulatory delay | City-level task force with East/Central priority |
| Festive inventory mismatch | Weekly colour×model pipeline review |
| Talent attrition in SC role | Referral bonus + accelerated certification path |
| Macro credit tightening | Expand captive finance co-brand offers |

## 1. Purpose

This document sets **approved retail volume and EV mix targets** by zone for FY2026 (Apr-2026 – Mar-2027). Targets are **planning numbers** for MBR reviews — actuals come from `fact_sales`. Do not confuse these targets with glossary metric definitions.

## 2. National FY2026 headline targets

| Metric | FY2026 target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Total retail units (India) | 1,42,000 units | Excludes export |
| YoY growth | +8.5% vs FY2025 | Baseline FY2025: 1,30,900 units |
| EV share (national) | 11.5% of retail units | Measured per SOP-003 |
| Revenue (INR crores) | 18,400 Cr | ASP assumption ₹13.0 L |

## 4. H1 vs H2 phasing

National phasing is **46% H1 / 54% H2** to reflect festive concentration:

- **H1 (Apr–Sep):** 65,320 units  
- **H2 (Oct–Mar):** 76,680 units — includes Diwali + year-end  

Zones may shift ±3% between halves with CSO approval; national total fixed.

## 6. Make mix guardrails (national)

Not hard targets — **review triggers** when MBR variance exceeds band:

| Make cluster | Share band (FY2026 plan) |
|---|---|
| Maruti + Hyundai | 38–42% combined |
| Tata + Mahindra | 28–32% combined |
| Toyota + Kia + Honda | 18–22% combined |
| Others | Balance |

Single-make swing > 4 pp vs plan in a zone → require competitive loss narrative in MBR.

## 8. How to use with analytics

When a user asks *“Are we on track for North?”*:

1. Pull **actual units YTD** from data by `region_name = North`  
2. Compare to **prorated target** from this document (zone annual × months elapsed / 12)  
3. Cite **IND-PV-REG-001** for the target number — not the glossary  

When a user asks *“What is the East target?”* with no data needed — answer **directly from this document**.

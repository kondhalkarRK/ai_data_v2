Business Problem
▪Insights depend on SQL experts and data teams, slowing business decisions.
▪Multiple definitions of key metrics create conflicting answers and erode trust.
▪Enterprise data is siloed, making discovery and analysis difficult.
▪Existing AI platforms often require costly data movement and vendor lock-in.
▪Users lack confidence in AI answers due to poor transparency and data quality visibility.
Approach
▪Ask in plain English, get answers in seconds. No SQL, dashboards, or analyst dependency.
▪Create a single source of truth through a governed semantic layer for metrics, business terms, and relationships.
▪Bring AI to the data, not data to the AI, eliminating costly data movement and reducing governance risk.
▪Embed trust into every answer with explainability, lineage, data quality scores, and confidence indicators.
▪Optimize AI costs through intelligent model routing, using the right LLM for the right question.
▪Enable enterprise-scale self-service, empowering every employee to access data-driven insights securely.
VALUEDELIVERED
•Configurable, model-agnostic AI platform with intelligent LLM routing, governance, and cost controls.
•Admin console for AI usage monitoring, budget management, and policy enforcement.
•Only schema metadata (DDL) is shared with LLMs, minimizing data exposure risks.
•Supports fully private/local LLM deployments for maximum data security.
•Data Trust Center provides trust scores and data quality insights to improve answer confidence.
•Enables business users to self-serve analytics through natural language queries.
•Scales to 20K+ monthly questions for 100 users while maintaining low operational costs.
•Pay-per-question pricing reduces annual AI costs to $3.5K–$17.8K, significantly below comparable Power BI deployments.

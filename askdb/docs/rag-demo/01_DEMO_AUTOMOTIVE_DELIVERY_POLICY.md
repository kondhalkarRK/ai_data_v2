## 1. Meaning of delivery delay

A delivery delay occurs when the promised customer delivery date has passed and the vehicle has not been handed over. A booking awaiting an initial promised date is not classified as delayed under this demo policy.

This operational definition does not define warehouse revenue recognition, unit-sales measures, accounting dates or order-count calculations. AskMe's governed semantic catalog remains authoritative for those metrics.

## 2. Escalation procedure

1. The dealer coordinator records the order reference, promised delivery date, current stage and known blocker in the internal tracking system.
2. At two business days overdue, the coordinator notifies the regional operations lead.
3. At five business days overdue, the regional operations lead reviews the case with central logistics.
4. The coordinator gives the customer a confirmed update after the review. A tentative date must be labeled tentative.

Business days mean Monday through Friday, excluding the applicable regional holiday calendar. If that calendar is unavailable, do not invent an exact escalation date.

## 4. Customer commitments

The coordinator must not promise compensation, a discount, a guaranteed delivery date or automatic cancellation based solely on this escalation policy. Compensation and cancellation require separate approved policies, which are not supplied in this demo corpus.

INDIA AUTO DEALER NETWORK POLICY AND MARKET BRIEF - FY2026
Prepared by: Sales Strategy Office. Audience: Regional Managers, Dealer Principals.

1. KEY FINDINGS FY2026
SUV share of passenger-vehicle sales crossed 52 percent nationally. Compact SUV is the fastest growing segment, led by Tata Nexon, Hyundai Venue, Maruti Suzuki Brezza and Kia Sonet. Electric vehicles reached 4.8 percent of units, with Tata holding the largest EV share, followed by MG and Mahindra. Hatchback demand declined 9 percent year on year, especially Alto and WagonR in Tier-2 cities. South region (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad) delivered the highest revenue growth at 14 percent. East region (Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, Patna) missed target units by 11 percent.

2. DEALER GRADE POLICY
Grade A dealers: monthly volume above 120 units, minimum 6 salespeople, eligible for 1.5 percent extra trade margin and priority allocation of waiting-period models such as Mahindra XUV700, Thar Roxx and Toyota Innova Hycross. Grade B dealers: 60 to 120 units, minimum 3 salespeople, standard margin. Grade C dealers: below 60 units, placed on a 2-quarter improvement plan. A dealer that misses 80 percent of target units for two consecutive quarters is downgraded one grade.

3. DISCOUNT AND INCENTIVE POLICY
Maximum consumer discount is 4 percent of ex-showroom price on petrol and diesel cars and 2 percent on Electric vehicles. Year-end stock (previous model year) may receive up to 7 percent with regional manager approval. Salesperson incentive: INR 1,500 per unit up to 8 units a month, INR 2,500 per unit above 8 units, and a INR 5,000 bonus for every EV sold. Discounts above policy must be logged in the DMS with an approval code; unlogged discounts are recovered from dealer margin.

4. EV STRATEGY 2026
Target: EV share of 8 percent by March 2027. Priority cities: Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, Hyderabad, New Delhi and Chennai. Every Grade A dealer in these cities must install one 60 kW DC fast charger by Q3 FY2026. Focus models: Tata Nexon EV, Tata Punch EV, MG Windsor EV, Mahindra BE 6, Hyundai Creta Electric and BYD Atto 3. Main concerns: charging availability in Tier-2 cities, resale value uncertainty and battery warranty anxiety.

5. REGIONAL TARGETS AND CONCERNS
North (New Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Jaipur, Lucknow): target growth 8 percent; concern is diesel restrictions in Delhi NCR pushing buyers to CNG and hybrid. West (Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Surat): target growth 10 percent; concern is high inventory of sedans such as Honda City and Hyundai Verna. South: target growth 12 percent; concern is salesperson attrition above 30 percent in Bengaluru. East: target growth 9 percent; recovery plan includes 20 new Grade B outlets in Bihar and Odisha. Central (Indore, Bhopal, Nagpur): target growth 7 percent; concern is low finance penetration.

6. WARRANTY AND SERVICE RULES
Standard warranty is 3 years or 100,000 km on petrol and diesel vehicles. EV battery warranty is 8 years or 160,000 km. Extended warranty may be sold up to 5 years. Pre-delivery inspection must be completed within 48 hours of vehicle arrival. Customer complaints must be closed within 7 working days; dealers above 5 percent repeat complaints lose priority allocation.

7. KPI DEFINITIONS USED IN REVIEWS
Revenue: total sales value of all orders. Units Sold: total quantity of vehicles sold, not the count of orders. Average Selling Price (ASP): revenue divided by units sold. Target Achievement: units sold divided by target units for the same make and month. EV Penetration: electric units divided by total units. Dealer Productivity: units sold per active salesperson per month.

8. RISKS
Price increases of 2 to 3 percent in April due to new safety norms, possible semiconductor delays for premium SUVs such as Toyota Fortuner and Jeep Meridian, aggressive pricing by new entrants BYD and Citroen, and monsoon-season slowdown in July and August.

9. RECOMMENDATIONS
Shift dealer stock mix toward Compact SUV and EV models; run hatchback exchange offers in Tier-2 cities; add two EV-trained salespeople per Grade A dealer in priority cities; review Grade C dealers in East region by Q2; and tie quarterly incentives to target achievement instead of raw discount levels.

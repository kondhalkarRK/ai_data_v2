# Automotive Dealer Performance Brief — Q3 FY2025

## Scope
This brief covers North and West zone Hyundai and Toyota dealerships for passenger vehicles (SUV, sedan, hatchback).

## Key findings
1. West zone SUV revenue grew 18% year over year, led by Creta and Venue variants.
2. North zone hatchback volume declined 7% after a supply delay on the i20 petrol line.
3. Average selling price for mid-size SUVs rose from INR 14.2 lakh to INR 14.9 lakh.
4. Dealer NPS target remains 70. Platinum dealers must maintain NPS above 70 for rebate eligibility.
5. Inventory days on hand for electric vehicles (EV) in Bengaluru and Pune averaged 42 days.

## Settlement and warranty
- Warranty claim settlement SLA is 7 business days for standard mechanical claims.
- High-severity powertrain claims require regional steward review before payout.
- Parts return window for rejected warranty claims is 15 calendar days.

## Recommendations
- Prioritize Creta inventory replenishment in West zone before festive season.
- Investigate i20 petrol supply chain delay with the OEM logistics partner.
- Offer EV test-drive weekends in South zone cities to reduce 42-day inventory aging.
